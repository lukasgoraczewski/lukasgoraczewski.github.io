## About the game

Bee Hunt 3D is a puzzle game where you tap boxes to send bees out to clear voxels from a picture. Merging three same-colored boxes is a core part of the loop.

## My role

I worked as a level design consultant.

## Challenges and decisions

- **Problem:** early tests showed weak retention, and the task of improving it landed with me.
- **Decision:** I doubled the number of bees released after each merge. This shifted the focus from clearing the picture to chasing merges.
- **Effect:** day-one retention went from 22% to 38%.
