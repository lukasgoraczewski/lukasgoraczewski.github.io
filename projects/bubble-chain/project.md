## About the game

Bubble Chain is a physics-based puzzle where players swipe to rotate a tower of colored spheres, then release to fire a harpoon that snags a connected chain of matching-colored bubbles. It was a prototype tested with Voodoo and has since been withdrawn.

## My role

I did the concept, game design, level design, balance and graphics.
